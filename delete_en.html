Account & Data Deletion

BudgetPuu | Developer: HiddenMaple

Important Notice

BudgetPuu has no backend server — the developer does not hold and cannot access any of your bookkeeping data or account data. Your data exists only on your device and, if you enable cloud sync, in your own Google Drive / Apple iCloud storage. "Deleting your account and data" is therefore something you do entirely on your own device and your own cloud account.

How to Delete Your Data

  1. Delete your account (cloud backups and local data at once): In the app, go to Settings → Account & backup → tap your account → Sign out / Delete account → choose Delete account; or Settings → Account & backup → Clear data → choose Full reset (device & cloud) (shown only while signed in). After you confirm, the app deletes all of its backups (including version history) stored in your own Google Drive / iCloud, clears all bookkeeping data on this device, and signs you out. If you signed in with Google, the app’s access to your Google Drive is revoked as well.
  2. Delete local data only: Settings → Account & backup → Clear data → choose what to clear; or simply uninstall the app from your phone (this clears all local data). Cloud backups are kept.
  3. Sign out only: Settings → Account & backup → tap your account → Sign out / Delete account → choose Sign out to end your Google / Apple sign-in (local data and cloud backups are kept; only future syncing stops).
  4. Remove Sign in with Apple permission (if you signed in with Apple): iPhone Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple → select this app → Stop Using Apple Account.

If several devices are signed in to the same account with sync turned on, delete the account or sign out on each of them; otherwise another device will upload its data to your cloud storage again the next time it syncs. You can also delete the app’s data directly in your own Google Drive (Settings → Manage apps) or iCloud (Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage).

What Gets Deleted, What Stays

  • After following the steps above, the corresponding bookkeeping data (accounts, categories, transaction records) and sign-in information will be deleted.
  • The developer keeps no server-side copy whatsoever; cloud backups are stored in your own cloud account, entirely under your control.
  • Once the app is uninstalled, local data is cleared immediately, with no additional retention period.
