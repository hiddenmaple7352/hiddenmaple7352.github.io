Privacy Policy

BudgetPuu | Version 1.0.2 | Last revised: 2026-10-28

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the "BudgetPuu" app (the "App") handles your personal data. Please read this policy carefully before using the App.

2. Scope of Data Collection

The App does not run its own server. The developer cannot access your bookkeeping data and never sends it to any advertiser, analytics service, or other third party.

All of your bookkeeping data (accounts, categories, transaction records) is stored only in:

  1. Your local device; and
  2. If you choose to enable cloud sync, your own Google Drive dedicated app folder (appDataFolder, readable/writable only by this app and counted against your own Drive quota), or your own Apple iCloud storage.

The following situations are triggered only by your own action, and involve third-party services:

Optional sign-in: If you choose to sign in with a Google or Apple account, the App stores your account identifier and, if provided, your email and display name locally — solely to identify the cloud sync destination. This is never sent to the developer's server.

Cloud sync: After signing in, if you enable cloud sync, the App writes a snapshot of your bookkeeping data to your own Google Drive appDataFolder or iCloud container. Your data always stays in your own cloud account and the developer cannot read it. Disabling sync or deleting the snapshot removes the cloud data.

Advertising (Free edition only): The Free edition embeds the Google AdMob SDK, which — as part of its normal operation — collects the device advertising identifier, IP address (for approximate region), device model, operating system, and similar information to serve ads. This data is held and managed by Google under Google's own privacy policy; the developer cannot access it. AdMob is disabled entirely once you purchase the Full edition, and no further data is collected.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services. Data related to each is managed solely by that service under its own privacy policy; the developer neither holds nor resells it:

  • Google Sign-In / Sign in with Apple: Third-party sign-in authentication (triggered only when you choose to sign in).
  • Google Drive API (appDataFolder) / Apple iCloud: Cloud sync access to your own cloud storage (triggered only when you enable sync; the data belongs to you).
  • Google AdMob: Banner ad delivery (Free edition only; disabled after purchasing the Full edition).
  • Apple App Store / Google Play in-app purchases: Handles the purchase and restoration of the one-time full-version license.

The App never sends your bookkeeping content (amounts, items, notes) to the advertising or analytics services above.

4. Data Storage Location

Local: All bookkeeping data is stored in a local SQLite database on your device.

Your own cloud (only when sync is enabled): Stored as a JSON snapshot in your own Google Drive appDataFolder or Apple iCloud container, owned by you; the developer cannot read it.

The App holds no server-side copy of any user data.

5. Data Backup and Export

The App provides a JSON export feature so you can back up your own data. The exported file is saved to a location you choose and is managed entirely by you.

6. Data Deletion

You can delete all of the App's data in the following ways:

  1. Delete account (cloud backups and local data at once): Settings → Account & backup → tap your account → Sign out / Delete account → choose Delete account
  2. Delete local data only: Settings → Account & backup → Clear data → choose what to clear; or uninstall the App directly (this clears all local data)

Deleting cloud data: Delete account above, or Clear data → Full reset (device & cloud) (shown only while signed in), deletes all of the App's backups (including version history) stored in your own Google Drive / iCloud. You can also delete them manually from your Drive / iCloud management interface. See Account & Data Deletion for step-by-step instructions.

Sign out / unlink: Settings → Account & backup → tap your account → Sign out / Delete account → choose Sign out to end your sign-in session. Local data and cloud backups are kept; only future syncing stops.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be revised as the App's features are updated. The version number and revision date are shown at the top of this document. Material changes will be announced within the App.
