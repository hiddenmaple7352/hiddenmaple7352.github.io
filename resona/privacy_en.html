Privacy Policy

Version: 1.0

Last updated: 2026-11-03

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Resona app (the "App") handles your data. Please read it before using the App.

2. What We Collect

The App does not connect to the internet, has no servers, and uses no advertising, analytics, or tracking services. The developer cannot access any of your data in the App.

Everything you create in the App is stored only on your device, including:

  • Bowl information (name, pitch, size, custom fields, tags, etc.)
  • Photos of your bowls that you take or select
  • Recordings and tuning records
  • App settings (language, reference pitch, appearance, etc.)

3. How Device Permissions Are Used

The App asks for these permissions only when you use the related feature, and all data is processed on your device:

  • Microphone: to analyze the frequency of your crystal singing bowl in real time and to record its sound. Audio is never uploaded.
  • Camera: to take a photo of a bowl in your collection.
  • Photos: to choose a photo for a bowl. The App only reads the photo you select.

You can turn these permissions off at any time in your device's Settings. The related feature will stop working; everything else is unaffected.

4. Export and Sharing

The App can export bowl information, photos, and recordings as a .resona file. You decide where the exported file is saved or who it is shared with (for example, saving it to Files or sending it to a friend). Once a file leaves the App, it is handled under the rules of the service or person you chose, which the App cannot control.

5. Deleting Your Data

  • Deleting a bowl, photo, recording, or tuning record in the App removes it from your device.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all of its data from your device.
  • To keep a copy before deleting, use the export feature first.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised as the App is updated. The version and date are shown at the top. If a future feature sends data off your device (such as cloud backup or in-app purchases), this policy will be updated first.
